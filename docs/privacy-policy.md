# WallKraft Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** (enter date)

WallKraft is a wallpaper browsing app. Your privacy is the default: the app
does not have accounts, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or
third-party tracking of any kind.

## Data we store

**Wallhaven API key (optional).** If you enter a Wallhaven API key in
Settings, it is stored **only on your device** (in Android's private app
storage). It is sent only to Wallhaven's API servers, as authentication for
the requests you make in the app. It is never uploaded anywhere else, and it
is excluded from Android cloud backup.

**Favorites.** Wallpapers you favorite are stored locally on your device.
They are not transmitted anywhere.

## Data we do NOT collect

- No accounts or registration
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
- No advertising SDKs
- No tracking, fingerprinting, or cross-app identification
- No collection of personal information, contacts, location, or device data
- We do not sell or share any data, because we collect none

## Network requests

The app talks only to the Wallhaven API (`wallhaven.cc`) to fetch wallpaper
listings, search results, and images. No other parties are contacted.

## Children

The app requests SFW content only and is suitable for all ages.
