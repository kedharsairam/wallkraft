WallKraft — Private wallpapers, crafted.

A fast, offline-first wallpaper app for Android powered by Wallhaven. No ads. No analytics. No trackers. Your data never leaves your device.

BROWSE
Masonry grid with adaptive columns, pull-to-refresh, skeleton loading, and prefetch. Shared-element hero transitions from tile to detail. Browse thousands of high-quality wallpapers from Wallhaven.

SEARCH & FILTER
Text search with history and suggestions. Filter by category, purity (SFW/Sketchy), orientation, color, and sorting. All filters persist between sessions.

DETAIL
Full-bleed, edge-to-edge viewing behind the status bar. Pinch to zoom with 3-step double-tap (fit, fill, native). View tags, uploader info, and stats. Share wallpapers via FileProvider.

SET WALLPAPER
Crop and zoom with frame preview. Set to Home screen, Lock screen, or Both. Remembered framing for quick reapply. Showcase and Atmosphere blur previews.

FAVORITES
Save full-res offline copies. Organize into custom collections with covers and counts. Multi-select and batch remove. Offline re-validation ensures favorites always work.

ROTATION
Auto-rotate favorites or any collection on a schedule (Hourly, Daily, Weekly). Manual rotate now. Showcase blur during transitions.

PRIVACY
No accounts required. Your Wallhaven API key (optional) lives in the Android Keystore and never leaves the device. Excluded from cloud backup. Sent only as X-API-Key header to Wallhaven. Favorites and downloads are stored locally with atomic writes. Never uploaded.

RELIABLE
Offline-first architecture. Search cache with 30-minute TTL and stale fallback. Favorites stored on disk with atomic writes. WorkManager chain for background tasks — no polling, boundary-aligned, battery-conscious. Every network call returns a Result type — no blank screens, no silent failures.
